The Appleseed Cast

Low Level Owl: Volumes I + II

Every memory of *Mare Vitalis*, again. *Kid A* came out around this time and I listened to these two incessantly. But also: remembering the full show I saw in Chicago with Kim on what must have been the 10 year anniversary tour, and discovering that Isaiah Sheese loves this album, too. Learning to play "Mile Marker", that lovely D shape chord, the pull-offs, the major third on the D string with the pinky finger. My concept of what music could be growling a little at a time. I still try to play music like these guys, i think. I love this album the whole way through, a real life sound track album for sure.
